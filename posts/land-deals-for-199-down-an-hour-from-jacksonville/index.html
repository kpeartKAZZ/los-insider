Artis called me from New Orleans. He wants land close to his daughter in Jacksonville, with a grandbaby on the way.

He had good questions. Most land buyers do. If you have some too, start with this short.

Artis wants to be set up by spring. Half an acre to an acre would be great. A mobile home or a house.

Here is the honest part. A home goes in once the land is paid off. So if you want to be on your land soon, pick a lot you can pay off fast. Pay it off early and you pay the cash price, not the full payment total.

If you want to be close to Jacksonville —

Federal Point — 0.27 acres, minutes from the St. Johns River. It is the closest lot I have to Jacksonville, about 45 minutes to an hour. A mobile home or a house can go here. Cash price $12,997. $199 down, $206/mo.

Federal Point. 0.27 acres near the St. Johns River. Cash price $12,997.

If you want more room —

Acorn Ct, Interlachen — two lots sold as one, almost half an acre. Power is at the street. Room for a mobile home now and more later. Cash price $13,997. $499 down, $257/mo.

Acorn Ct, Interlachen. Almost half an acre. Power at the street.

Interlachen Lakes Estates — a quarter acre with power at the street. The boat ramp on Lake Grandin is about 10 minutes away. Cash price $13,997. $199 down, $219/mo.

Artis asked one more smart question. What does it cost to clear the trees? Every lot is different, so that is a great one to ask on a call.
