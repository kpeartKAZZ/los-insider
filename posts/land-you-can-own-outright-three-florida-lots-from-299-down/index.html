A Full Acre in Marianna — Just over an acre on a quiet street inside town. Groceries, the hospital and hardware are minutes out, not a half hour.

How the discount works.

There is no interest and no credit check. Every payment goes to principal.

There is no penalty for paying early. Pay the balance inside 12 months and the cash price is what you owe.

Once it is paid off, the deed goes into your name. That deed is what the county wants before it will issue a building permit.
