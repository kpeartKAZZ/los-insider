Build It Your Way ☀️ Land for a Tiny or Container Home

Published by Lots of Sunshine · FL Florida Land Arkansas Land Owner Financing Land Investing

Shameka called me this week. She is selling her house and she wants land she can build on her way. A tiny home. A container home. Maybe a mobile home. Not a stick built house because somebody said so.

She also asked me a smart question. How do you know the land is really mine to sell?

Watch the 30-second answer →

Then I pulled three lots for her. All three let you build. None of them are waiting on the county to open permits. Down payments start at $199.

The one with the most room

0.38 Acres in Interlachen — The quiet end of a wooded street. Almost four tenths of an acre.

Put a container home, a tiny home or a mobile home on it. Room left over for the driveway, the septic and a yard. No HOA. Palatka is about twenty minutes away.

$249 down, $330 a month for 72 months. Cash price $18,997.

Interlachen — oaks, moss and quiet. $249 down.

The one near the clear water

0.24 Acres in Dunnellon — Paved roads. Power at the street. No HOA. The land is high and dry.

Build a house, a modular or a tiny home. The Rainbow River is minutes away and the water is so clear you can watch the sandy bottom go by. Ocala is thirty minutes.

$299 down, $301 a month for 72 months. Cash price $18,997.

Dunnellon — paved roads, high and dry, thirty minutes to Ocala. $299 down.

The easiest one to start

0.22 Acres in Interlachen — Three minutes from the main road, right in the middle of town.

Mobile home, tiny home, container home. All three are allowed here, so you do not have to call anybody to find out. You can even put up two doors and rent one side.

$199 down, $182 a month for 84 months. Cash price $12,997.

One more thing Shameka asked about. Pay the balance off within twelve months of your down payment and you pay the cash price, not the terms total. No penalty. That is real money back in your pocket.

It also gets you the deed faster, and the deed is what the county wants before it will hand you a building permit.
