Acres Near the Gulf ☀️ $499 Down and $339 a Month

Published by Lots of Sunshine · MULTI Florida Land Arkansas Land Owner Financing Land Investing

Ninety minutes. That is the drive from these four Panhandle lots to the white sand. Close enough that the beach is a Saturday, not a vacation.

Before you scroll, meet Gary. He is technically the listing agent.

Watch the 60-second short →

The drive is easy. Panama City is about an hour. The beach is 90 minutes. I-10 runs right past Marianna, so you are on a highway the whole way.

Close to home you get caves you can walk through, four miles of clear blue spring water, and some of the best fishing in the state. Town is minutes away.

And these lots are big. Three of the four are over an acre.

If you want the water out your door —

1.17 Acres on Desoto Street, Round Lake — Spring country, fifteen minutes north of Marianna. The water runs 68 degrees all year and is clear enough to snorkel. Kayak in the morning, surf by lunch. $499 down, $351/mo.

1.17 acres on Desoto Street · spring water minutes away · $499 down.

A Third of an Acre on Round Lake — The easiest one to walk into. A quiet 30-acre lake, and this lot sits right on the edge of it. Fish it in the evening. $499 down, $339/mo.

A third of an acre on Round Lake · the lowest payment of the four · $339/mo.

If you want room and no one telling you what to build —

1.74 Acres on Pisgah Court, Round Lake — The biggest of the four. Build a house, bring a manufactured home, a mobile home, or a tiny home. That choice stays yours. $749 down, $388/mo.

1.74 acres on Pisgah Court · the biggest lot of the four · $749 down.

A Full Acre on Lilac Court, Marianna — Paved road, utilities close, town right there. Park the RV while you plan the build. $749 down, $348/mo.
