Land Deals an Hour From the Beach ☀️ $499 Down

Published by Lots of Sunshine · FL Florida Land Arkansas Land Owner Financing Land Investing Market Updates

An hour up US-231 from the sugar-white sand of the Gulf, the ground around Marianna opens into caves and the springs run 68 degrees every day of the year. I just put four lots up there, and they are the biggest ones I have listed in a while.

Here is what these four Panhandle lots have in common. I-10 runs right past Marianna and US-90 and US-231 cross in town, so you are never hunting for a way in. Panama City sits 58 miles down that same highway, a little over an hour, and the beach is another fifteen minutes past it. Every one of them is zoned residential with room to move, which means a site-built home, a manufactured home, a mobile home, or a tiny home are all welcome. And down payments start at $499.

If you want to live minutes from the only walk-through caves in the state —

A Full Acre on Lilac Court, Marianna — Just over an acre on a quiet residential street, with paved roads and utilities close and enough ground left over for a garden, a workshop, and a place to park the boat. Florida Caverns State Park is under five miles away. Groceries, hardware, and the hospital are a few minutes in the other direction. $749 down, $348/mo.

A full acre on Lilac Court in Marianna · caverns and cold springs minutes away · $749 down.

If you want to live on the water —

1.17 Acres in Round Lake — Over an acre in spring country, fifteen minutes north of Marianna. Merritt's Mill Pond runs four and a half miles of water so clear that people snorkel it, and the Chipola River is right there for a lazy float. Bring the kayak, build when you are ready. $499 down, $351/mo.

A Third of an Acre by the Lake in Round Lake — The smallest lot here and the easiest to walk into. Round Lake itself is a quiet 30-acre freshwater lake, and this one sits on the edge of it. Fish it in the evening from land that belongs to you. $499 down, $339/mo.

1.17 acres in Round Lake · spring-fed water and clear rivers all around · $499 down.

If you want the room to build a forever home —

1.74 Acres on Pisgah Court, Round Lake — The biggest lot of the four. Put the house where you want it, sink your own well, run your own septic, and still have room for shade trees and a workshop out back. Morning coffee on your own porch with nothing but birdsong to move you along. $749 down, $388/mo.

And here is the part nobody else will give you —

You can buy any lot on this list with low money down. That is the whole point of how we do this. No bank, no credit check, no waiting on an underwriter to decide what your life looks like. Just low down payments and easy monthly payments, the same terms I would want if I were the one buying. I have not seen anyone else in this business offer financing like it. It is one of the ways we make buying land easy and affordable.
