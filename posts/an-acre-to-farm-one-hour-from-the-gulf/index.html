An Acre to Farm ☀️ One Hour From the Gulf

Published by Lots of Sunshine · FL Florida Land Arkansas Land Owner Financing Land Investing

Roma called me from Key West, Florida. After almost 20 years of renting a condo, Roma wants an acre to farm, about an hour from the Gulf.

Some buyers already know their spot. This one picked a river near Jacksonville.

Watch the 60-second river lots video →

Back to Roma. The plan is fruit trees, a vegetable garden, and a house built over time.

The wish list was short. An acre or more. The Gulf close enough for a morning swim. A real town nearby.

Here are three lots I sent Roma. Each one is over an acre.

Pisgah Ct, Round Lake — 1.74 acres. Room for orange trees, a garden, and your home. Panama City Beach is about an hour south. Marianna is fifteen minutes away. $749 down, $388/mo.

Pisgah Ct, Round Lake. 1.74 acres. $749 down.

Lake County Blvd, Jennings — 1.2 acres of farm land. Grow food, raise animals, and build almost any kind of home. Valdosta is about twenty minutes north. The Gulf is a longer drive from here. $499 down, $411/mo.

Desoto St, Round Lake — 1.17 acres, with the lake close by for fishing. The same easy drive to the Gulf. The lowest payment of the three. $499 down, $351/mo.
