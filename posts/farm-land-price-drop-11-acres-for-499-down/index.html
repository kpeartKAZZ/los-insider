Kenya asked us two good questions. Do you have anything in South Florida? And is it rural?

If you want to grow your own food —

NW Blue Oak Ln, Jennings — 1.1 acres of farm land. Grow food, raise a few animals, and build a home. The cash price just dropped $10,000. This is the one that fits Kenya's number. $17,997 cash, or $499 down and $280/mo.
